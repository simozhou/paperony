# Weekly literature report — prompt

Paste this prompt, then attach or paste this week's `literature_*.json` (or `.compact.json`).

---

I'm a researcher looking for a postdoc position. My background is single-cell genomics, gene regulatory networks, developmental biology, and sequence-to-function models. The attached JSON contains this week's research articles from the journals I follow (fetched from PubMed) plus new bioRxiv preprints that were pre-filtered for my interests.

Use only the information in the JSON. Don't invent findings, authors, or affiliations; if something is missing, say so. Cite each paper by title with its URL.

Write the report in these sections:

1. **The week at a glance.** A few paragraphs on the main themes across all journal articles, grouped by topic rather than by journal. Mention notable papers briefly, including ones outside my field.

2. **Relevant to my background.** For every paper or preprint that genuinely touches my areas (use `relevance.themes` as a starting hint, but judge from the abstract), give:
   - a 2–3 sentence summary of the question, approach and main result;
   - which of my areas it connects to and why;
   - the last author and their affiliation (and the corresponding author for preprints, if different);
   - whether it's a published paper or a preprint.
   Order by how relevant the paper is to me, most relevant first.

3. **Postdoc project ideas.** 3–5 concrete project ideas that build on this week's papers: what open question each addresses, which of my skills it uses, and which labs from this week's list would be a natural fit to host it.

4. **Labs to watch.** A short table of last authors from this week who work closest to my interests, with affiliation and the paper(s) that put them on the list. Note any lab that appears more than once.
